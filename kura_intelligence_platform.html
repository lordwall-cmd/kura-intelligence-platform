Which disinfectant should I use against the ST6 strain in raw material reception?
The ST6 isolates from samples AA-028, AA-034 and 2 drain samples carry qacH and bcrABC genes — conferring resistance to benzalkonium chloride at standard concentration.

Recommended action: use hydrogen peroxide at 0.5% or quaternary ammonium at ≥1000 ppm. Standard quaternaries (200 ppm) will not be effective.

The strain shows persistence markers across 3 sample dates — review CIP procedures alongside disinfectant change.

Key findings
Interpretation by Bastián B. · Codebreaker Bioinformatics
⚠ ST6 — Immediate action required
The ST6 strain present in raw material reception and drain D-04 carries qacH and bcrABC resistance genes. These confer reduced susceptibility to benzalkonium chloride-based sanitizers at standard plant concentration (200 ppm).

The standard CIP protocol is likely ineffective for this strain. Until disinfectant is changed, ST6 will persist regardless of cleaning frequency.
◆ ST121 — Review operator hygiene + disinfectant
The ST121 strain on the filleting line carries qacA, conferring low-level resistance to low-dose quaternary ammonium compounds. The probable source is an operator with insufficient handwashing.

Recommend: increase hand disinfectant concentration at line entry + mandatory boot-bath with peracetic acid ≥200 ppm.
